## 2. Clinical Standards — What a Real Treatment Record Contains

### Cancer Research UK "Your Treatment Record" (gold standard)

**Structure:** Diagnosis → Treatment plan → Cycle records → Side effects table → Traffic light assessment

**Key features:**
- Traffic light severity: GREEN (mild) / AMBER (moderate) / RED (severe)
- Cycle-by-cycle tracking: drug names, dates, dose, cycle number
- Blood test tracking: WBC, ANC, platelets, hemoglobin, creatinine, LFTs
- Side effects checklist (18 symptoms) rated 0–3 each week
- Emergency triage guidance (when to call 24hr line)
- Sepsis awareness box

**UKONS Triage Tool symptoms (standard):**
1. Temperature (>37.5°C or <36°C)
2. Generally unwell / flu-like
3. Signs of infection (shivery, rigors)
4. Tiredness/fatigue
5. Numbness/pins & needles
6. Bleeding or bruising
7. Diarrhoea
8. Nausea/vomiting
9. Sore mouth
10. Pain
11. Skin rash/nail changes
12. Constipation
13. Loss of appetite
14. Eye problems
15. Sore hands and feet
16. Change in behaviour
17. Urinary problems
18. Mood changes

### NCI PRO-CTCAE (Patient-Reported Outcomes)
- Severity: None / Mild / Moderate / Severe
- Frequency: Never / Rarely / Occasionally / Frequently / Almost constantly
- Interference with daily activities
- Validated instrument used in clinical trials worldwide

### Quebec Oncology Passport (Passeport en oncologie)
- Personal info + health snapshot
- Healthcare team directory
- Treatment plan: surgery, chemo, radiation
- Weight and blood tests tracking
- Central venous catheter management
- Symptom list with severity scale
- Medication list (including supplements)
- Life after cancer section

## 3. Design Best Practices

### What Top Journals Get Right

1. **Traffic light severity system** — not just "1–10" but color-coded urgency (UKONS standard)
2. **Pre-labeled checkboxes** — don't make patients invent categories while fatigued
3. **Cycle-by-cycle tracking** — one page per cycle, not cramped grids
4. **Blood test tracking** — WBC, ANC, platelets, hemoglobin (most competitors skip this)
5. **"What helped" notes** — patients want to remember what worked each cycle
6. **Emergency guidance** — clear "when to call" boxes with specific thresholds
7. **Gift market design** — "Messages from Loved Ones" pages, "given to me by" line

### What Competitors Get Wrong

1. **Blank unlabeled checkboxes** — forces executive function when patients have "chemo brain"
2. **Cramped cycle planners** — 4–6 mini grids per page = unusable writing space
3. **No lab tracking** — patients track WBC/ANC themselves between appointments
4. **Pink-ribbon default** — not all chemo patients have breast cancer
5. **Generic severity scale** — 1–10 doesn't communicate urgency
6. **No emergency triage** — patients don't know when to call vs. when it can wait
7. **No insurance/billing section** — logistics that eat mental energy

### Color System (from our spec — validated against research)

| Role | Color | Hex | Purpose |
|------|-------|-----|---------|
| Tracking pages | Muted sage | `#8CA396` | Calm, clinical without being cold |
| Emotional support | Dusty blush | `#E3B8B0` | Warmth, human connection |
| Reference/logistics | Soft slate blue | `#A9BFC9` | Information, structure |
| Text | Warm charcoal | `#3F3A36` | Readable, not harsh black |
| Gridlines | Warm taupe | `#D8D0C7` | Subtle, doesn't overwhelm |
| Background | Warm ivory | `#FBF7F1` | Comforting, not clinical white |

### Typography

| Element | Font | Size | Notes |
|---------|------|------|-------|

### Emergency Contacts — Enhanced Callout Box

**RED — Call immediately:**
- Temperature above 37.5°C (99.5°F) or below 36°C (96.8°F)
- Uncontrolled bleeding or unusual bruising
- Severe shortness of breath
- Signs of allergic reaction (swelling, difficulty breathing, rash)
- Confusion or fainting
- Pain not relieved by medication
- Persistent vomiting (unable to keep fluids down)
- Signs of infection (shivering, rigors, even with normal temperature)

**AMBER — Call within 24 hours:**
- Mild fever (37.0–37.5°C)
- Diarrhoea (4+ episodes in 24 hours)
- Sore mouth affecting eating
- Skin rash (not severe)
- Mild bleeding/bruising
- New or worsening symptoms

**GREEN — Tell care team at next appointment:**
- Mild fatigue
- Mild nausea (managed with medication)
- Taste changes
- Hair loss
- Mild constipation
- Mood changes
