## Pricing & Royalty

**KDP paperback royalty:** Amazon marketplaces = list × 60% − printing cost.

**Printing cost** (US, B&W, >108pp) ≈ $1.00 + $0.012 × pages → **$2.56** for 130 pages.

| List | Channel | Formula | Net royalty |
|---|---|---|---|
| $14.99 | Amazon 60% | 0.60×14.99 − 2.56 | **$6.43** |
| $12.99 | Amazon 60% | 0.60×12.99 − 2.56 | $5.23 |
| $16.99 | Amazon 60% | 0.60×16.99 − 2.56 | $7.63 |

Recommended list: **$14.99** (higher perceived value for a health/wellness journal with 130 pages of specialized content).

> **Withholding note:** Algeria has no US tax treaty, so 30% withholding applies to US-sourced royalties. Cash received ≈ $4.50 per sale at $14.99.

## Cover Design Notes

### Specs for Affinity Publisher / Canva

**Custom canvas (full wrap, 300 DPI):**
- **5205 × 3375 px** (= 17.35 × 11.25 in)
- **Bleed:** 37.5 px (0.125 in) inset on all four edges
- **Spine:** center strip **106 px** wide (0.353 in)
- Panels: back = left 2550 px, spine = center 106 px, front = right 2550 px (each panel + its outer bleed)

**Color palette (from content spec):**
- Page background: Warm ivory `#FBF7F1`
- Tracking pages accent: Muted sage `#8CA396`
- Emotional-support accent: Dusty blush `#E3B8B0`
- Reference/logistics accent: Soft slate blue `#A9BFC9`
- Text: Warm charcoal `#3F3A36`

**Cover design direction:**
- Front: Calming, clean — sage/slate tones, line-art patient illustrations (from package)
- Back: Brief description bullets, barcode zone clear (lower-right)
- Spine: "CHEMOTHERAPY TREATMENT JOURNAL" centered

**Export:** File → Download → PDF Print · include bleed · RGB (KDP converts to CMYK) · 300 DPI.

## KDP Upload Steps

1. Sign in at kdp.amazon.com (kaprikika8@gmail.com).
2. Create → **Paperback**.
3. **Details:** language English; title + subtitle; **Author = Meridian Press**; **Publisher = Meridian Press**; paste description HTML; "I own the copyright"; 7 keywords; 3 categories.
4. **Content:** choose KDP free ISBN; trim 8.5×11; **white** paper, B&W; no bleed; matte cover.
5. Upload **Chemo-Journal-Interior.pdf**, then the chosen **cover wrap**.
6. Launch **KDP Previewer**, resolve any warnings.
7. **Pricing:** US $14.99, 60% royalty; enable other marketplaces as desired.
8. Publish → review typically 24–72 h.
