## Pricing & Royalty (Corrected)

**KDP paperback royalty is NOT a flat 40%.** It is:

- **Amazon marketplaces:** list × **60%** − printing cost. Falls to **50%** if the list price is below the marketplace threshold (rule added June 2025).
- **Expanded Distribution:** list × **40%** − printing cost.

**Printing cost** (US, B&W, >108pp) ≈ $1.00 + $0.012 × pages → **$2.44** for 120 pages.

| List | Channel | Formula | Net royalty |
|---|---|---|---|
| $12.99 | Amazon 60% | 0.60×12.99 − 2.44 | **$5.35** |
| $12.99 | Exp. Dist. 40% | 0.40×12.99 − 2.44 | $2.76 |
| $11.99 | Amazon 60% | 0.60×11.99 − 2.44 | $4.75 |
| $14.99 | Amazon 60% | 0.60×14.99 − 2.44 | $6.55 |

Your earlier docs listed "40% = $5.20"; the net is close by coincidence, but the model was wrong and would misprice other books. Recommended list: **$12.99** (test $11.99–$14.99).



> **Two distinct numbers — keep them separate.** **$5.35** is the royalty KDP *reports* per Amazon sale. Because Algeria has no US tax treaty, 30% withholding applies to US sales, so cash actually *received* is ≈ **$3.75**. Use $5.35 for KDP reconciliation and $3.75 for cash-flow projections. (An earlier draft listed "~$2.35 per sale" — that figure is not reproducible from any KDP formula; discard it.)

## Cover & Canva Setup (Corrected DPI)

The delivered `cover-wrap.pdf` is already print-ready — upload it as-is. To rebuild or tweak in Canva:

**Custom canvas (full wrap, 300 DPI):**
- **3774 × 2775 px** (= 12.58 × 9.25 in). Your earlier note said 1256×925 px "@300 DPI" — that is only 100 DPI. Use 3774×2775.
- **Bleed:** 37.5 px (0.125 in) inset on all four edges.
- **Spine:** center strip **99 px** wide (0.3302 in); keep spine text ≥ ~19 px from each fold.
- Panels: back = left 1837 px, spine = center 99 px, front = right 1837 px (each panel + its outer bleed).

**Export:** File → Download → PDF Print · include bleed · RGB (KDP converts to CMYK) · 300 DPI.

Keep all text ≥ 0.25 in inside the trim; leave the lower-right of the back cover clear for KDP's barcode.

## Algeria — Tax & Payment

- **US tax:** Complete the KDP US tax interview (W-8BEN). Algeria has **no US tax treaty**, so a **30% withholding** applies to US-sourced royalties — this cannot be reduced by treaty.
- **Payment:** Your account is set to **check payments** (no EFT bank on file for Algeria). Amazon issues royalty checks once the per-marketplace threshold is met; consider consolidating marketplaces to reach thresholds faster.
- Keep the account name/address consistent with your tax forms to avoid payout holds.
